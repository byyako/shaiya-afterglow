# Episode 7

Little is documented publicly about Episode 7, and no client build labelled EP7 is on hand. This page collects what news sources say about the "Iron Invasion" update and states what is missing.

## In short

- **Iron Invasion.** A US news report on 2013-08-26 and a Korean report on 2013-08-01 describe an update built around a mechanical-monster horde (**ref**).
- **Added systems.** A level-based battleground in the Stable Erde zone, four world bosses, the Infinite Sanctuary dungeon, the Chaotic Square crafting system and a colour dye system (**ref**).
- **Level cap not stated.** No source read gives it. Level 80 weapons are mentioned, which fits the EP6 cap of 80 (*inferred*).
- **Korea ran ahead by at least four weeks** (*inferred* from two article dates; the Korean 08-01 article is already "Part 2"). How the Korean "Part 1" and "Part 2" map to the English "Episode Seven" is *unverified*.
- **No client on hand.** Nothing here comes from a binary.

### Using this page

- **If you write a server:** no EP7 server pack has been studied on this site. For nearby behaviour read [Episode 6](ep6.md) and the [EP5.4 server pack page](../../original/ep5.4.md).
- **If you patch or mod the client:** there is nothing EP7-specific to take from this page.

## What changed for players

- A mechanical-monster horde as the update's theme (**ref**).
- Stable Erde zone changed, with level-based battlegrounds; the Korean article names a battle zone for level 55 and up (**ref**).
- Four world bosses (**ref**).
- The Infinite Sanctuary dungeon, five levels of ten stages (**ref**).
- The Chaotic Square crafting system (**ref**).
- A colour dye system (**ref**). The December 2020 unification notice also lists dyeing among its Episode 8 features, so which episode introduced it is *unverified* (see [Episode 8](ep8.md)).
- Level cap, races and classes: not stated (—).
