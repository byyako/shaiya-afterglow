# The player guide

A plain-language guide to how the game's numbers work, for players. It describes the original game (the EP4.5 server and the US client),
as the rest of this site does, and says at the end of each page what Shaiya Afterglow does differently, if anything.

The guide gives every formula and constant, worked examples with real items and monsters, and the quirks a player would not guess. It
carries almost no addresses: each section ends with a link to the reference note it comes from, where the addresses and packets are.

## The pages

1. **[Your character's numbers](guide/character-numbers.md)**: the six stats, starting stats and points, HP, SP and MP, hit, evasion,
   attack, defence and critical, what equipment, lapis and enchants add, buffs and passives, speeds, recovery, the modes, the faction
   kill-count bonus, what the character window shows, and a level-60 fighter worked out in full.
2. **[Hitting and being hit](guide/hitting.md)**: the attack timer and casting, the hit chance, damage on monsters and the level gap,
   skills, monsters' blows on you, players against players, experience, and three worked fights.
3. **[Elements, resistances and defences](guide/elements-defences.md)**: the element table, defence against magic defence, resisting magic
   and debuffs, absorption, blocks and reflection, the lapis that cast skills on hits, and a monster blow with and without an elemental
   armour.

Start with the first page: it explains the words the other two use.

## Where the numbers come from

Every formula was read in the original server (`ps_game.exe`) or, for the character window, in the US client (`game.exe`), and is
written up with its address in the reference tabs. The worked examples use rows of the original's item and monster data and were checked
against a model of the formulas that follows Shaiya Afterglow's code.
